## What's wrong now
- `pip-audit` on the `uv.lock` export: 137 vulns in 12 packages — starlette 0.48.0, python-multipart 0.0.18, pyjwt 2.8.0, litellm 1.61.15, PyPDF2 3.0.1 (unmaintained), bokeh 3.5.1, transformers 4.53.0, torch 2.4.1, langchain*, sentence-transformers.
- GitHub Dependabot: 7 critical / 24 high open alerts on `core_backend/requirements.txt`, 3 high on `requirements-dev.txt`.

## What needs to change
- Upgrade fastapi/starlette, python-multipart, pyjwt, litellm, bokeh, transformers/torch, langchain*; replace PyPDF2 with `pypdf`.
- Record any accepted risk in the dated ignore list used by the CI audit job (C6).

## Done when
- `pip-audit` on the locked export shows no high/critical (or each is documented).
- Zero open critical/high Dependabot alerts on backend manifests (or documented); test suite green.
