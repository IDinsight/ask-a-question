## What's wrong now
- next@14.2.35 in `admin_app/pnpm-lock.yaml`. `pnpm audit --prod`: 31 vulns (3 critical incl. unauthenticated RCE in Image Optimization, 11 high incl. middleware bypass/SSRF), plus postcss path traversal. `next/image` is used in `src/app/(auth)/layout.tsx:1`.
- Dependabot shows 3 critical / 38 high on the old `package-lock.json` (deleted on this branch; alerts should move to pnpm-lock).

## What needs to change
- Upgrade to a patched release (audit says ≥ 15.5.28) with matching React and `next-runtime-env`.
- Stopgap until merged: `images.unoptimized` or block `/_next/image` at Caddy.
- Unlocks `forbidden()`/`unauthorized()` (15.1+) used by F15.

## Done when
- `pnpm audit --prod --audit-level high` exits 0.
- `pnpm build` and the Playwright suite pass.
