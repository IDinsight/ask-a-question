**Target:** dsem Level 3 · **Skill:** dsem-ci-cd

## What's wrong now
- No `concurrency:` or `timeout-minutes:` in any workflow. Deploys `docker stop/rm/run` on one VM (`deploy_gcp_core_backend.yaml:118-151`), so back-to-back merges can race.

## What needs to change
- CI: `group: ${{ github.workflow }}-${{ github.ref }}`, `cancel-in-progress: true`. Deploys: `group: deploy-<env>-<service>`, `cancel-in-progress: false`.
- Timeouts: ~10 min lint/build, 15–20 min tests (measure), 30 min deploy.

## Done when
- Every workflow has a concurrency group and every job a timeout; a second push to a PR cancels the first run.
