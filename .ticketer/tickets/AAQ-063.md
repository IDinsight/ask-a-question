**Target:** dsem Level 3 · **Skill:** dsem-ci-cd §5, dsem-security

## What's wrong now
- No workflow audits dependencies or scans for secrets; detect-secrets runs only in skippable local pre-commit (`.pre-commit-config.yaml`). Repo-level Dependabot security updates are on but nothing gates PRs.

## What needs to change
- `.github/workflows/security.yml` (`pull_request`, `push: main`, weekly `schedule`): `backend-audit` (`uv export --frozen | pip-audit -r /dev/stdin`), `frontend-audit` (`pnpm audit --prod --audit-level=high`), `secret-scan` (detect-secrets against `.secrets.baseline` or gitleaks).
- Dated ignore list for accepted findings.

## Done when
- Jobs run on PRs and schedule; a planted test secret or known-vulnerable pin fails the job.
- Green once S15 and S16 land.
