**Target:** dsem Level 3 · **Skill:** dsem-security

## What's wrong now
- `contents/schemas.py:14-19` has `max_length` commented out; `question_answer/schemas.py:38` `query_text` is unbounded.
- `docmuncher/routers.py:124-146` reads the whole upload into memory, calls `zip_file.read()` on every entry with no size/ratio check (zip bomb), accepts by extension only, and lets `PdfReader` errors bubble up as 500.

## What needs to change
- Restore `max_length` on content title/text; cap `query_text` (2–4k chars) and metadata size.
- docmuncher: configurable MB cap, `%PDF-` magic-byte check, cap zip entry count and sum of `ZipInfo.file_size` before reading, map `PdfReadError` to 400.

## Done when
- Negative tests: oversized body → 422; zip-bomb fixture → 400; non-PDF renamed `.pdf` → 400; truncated PDF → 400 (not 500).
